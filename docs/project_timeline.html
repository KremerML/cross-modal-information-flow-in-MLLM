Project chronology · reconstructed from git, docs, and result files

Cross-modal information flow in LLaVA-1.5-7b

Nine months of attention knockout and SAE ablation, told as the sequence of assumptions that were made, tested, and — five times — overturned.

19 Jan 2026 → 9 Aug 2026 129 commits 7 branches 18 null experiments, then a positive one 2 published claims retracted

The shape of the project. The knockout half worked from February onward and never really changed. Everything else is a chain of falsified assumptions about where to intervene, which features to pick, and what to compare against. Each error was only visible once the previous one was fixed, which is why the null streak ran to 18 experiments before anything moved.

Two of the five corrections came after the writeup phase had begun. Both changed conclusions that were already written down.

Four attempts to rescue the null, none of which touch the real cause

In order: a hyperparameter grid sweep (Codex branch, PR #1 — on the theory that the SAE was mistrained); reproducibility scaffolding and unit tests plus "a major fix to sae targets"; a failed run where the matched sampler emitted the same random set repeatedly; a pivot from residual → attn_out, reasoning that since the knockout blocks attention, the SAE should model the attention output; splitting the dataset by attribute type (color/material/shape/size/state) so a single SAE isn't asked to span all of them; and a second selection metric, absolute activation difference, to dodge the ratio's instability.

Every one produces binding ≈ random. The most rigorously controlled run of the era (rerun_layer11_attn_out, n=877, 15 control sets) returns empirical p = 1.0 on both accuracy and margin.

AssumedThe null is a tuning problem — wrong hyperparameters, wrong site, wrong attribute mix, or a slightly wrong scoring formula.
OverturnedMarch's ceiling experiment showed the intervention was physically undetectable at the chosen positions, so no amount of tuning inside that design could have worked.

Worth noting for the writeup: the random-control sampler misbehaved as early as 9 Feb and was patched. The same subsystem silently failed again for the entire published run, undetected until August (Phase 8).

Ask what the maximum possible effect is, before asking why the effect is zero

The pivotal methodological move of the project: 10_full_latent_ablation.py zeroes all features to establish an upper bound on how much the SAE can possibly perturb the model.

At attribute positions the SAE reconstruction is 0.3% of the activation norm — ablating there is invisible by construction. At all positions the SAE captures essentially the whole norm and reproduces the knockout's margin drop. The dictionary was never the problem; the position was.

The same week produces the six-cause post-mortem in CLAUDE.md (position mismatch · single-layer vs. persistent barrier · ~6–8k training vectors for 32,768 features · selection confounded with task difficulty · language-side bypass in ChooseAttr · missing SAE best practices) and the GQA dataset audit that finds only color (602 rows) usable — shape has 15 rows, state is 77% generic "choose X|Y" items, 20.6% of color objects cover under 1% of the image.

Plans A, C, D follow: standardise every config to replace mode; add an image position type; and fix the SAE architecture (learnable b_pre, decoder column normalisation, dead-feature tracking, cosine LR, 32768→4096 features, l1 1e-3→5e-4).

New assumptionIntervene at image token positions — the source. Corrupt the signal there and no downstream layer can re-read it, which is the only way to match the knockout's persistent barrier.
OverturnedThree weeks later, and exactly backwards — see Phase 4.

Two well-designed experiments that were null by construction

Plan E (knockout_guided_features.py) drops correctness-based selection entirely and ranks features by how much the layer-0 knockout changes them — supervision straight from the causal intervention. Plan F (activation patching) replaces zeroing with patching in counterfactual knockout values, so downstream layers receive the corrupted signal rather than a hole they can route around.

Both return exactly zero. Feature scores at image positions: max = mean = p95 = 0.00000.

The inversionLLaVA is decoder-only with image tokens prepended, so the causal mask means image tokens never receive anything from the question. An Image→Question knockout changes only what the question tokens receive; image-token activations propagate through all 32 layers untouched. Measuring at image positions was measuring the wrong side of the transfer. Patching zeros into zeros changes nothing.
Replaced byIntervene at question token positions — the destination is the only place the transferred signal exists. The radio-signal framing in the May update: you analyse at the antenna, not the transmitter.

A second finding compounds it: the V2 SAE had been trained on all positions, which are ~90% image tokens, so it could barely reconstruct question-token activations at all (0.14% relative perturbation). The training position and the intervention position had drifted apart.

Two branches open here. paligemma-2 (4 Apr, never merged) ports the whole pipeline to PaliGemma-2 3B so it can use pretrained GemmaScope SAEs — the reasoning being that SAEs trained on billions of tokens sidestep both the training-scale and dead-feature problems at once. And CLEVR-Lite (6 Apr) replaces GQA: synthetic 6 colors × 3 shapes, open-ended queries. One change fixes three diagnosed causes — no language-side bypass, unlimited clean data, and no tiny-object ambiguity.

v2: gradient × activation — the 18-null streak breaks

The diagnosis that finally lands is about the metric, not the plumbing. The v1 ratio (correct_mean + 1e-8)/(incorrect_mean + 1e-8) is maximised by features that barely fire: 176 of 200 selected features had incorrect_mean = 0, and the top-ranked feature had correct_mean = 1.22e-05 against a residual-stream norm of ~5.5. Ghost features. Ablating them perturbs the model by ~0.0001%.

v2 inserts the SAE into the forward pass, detaches feature activations into a leaf tensor (detach().requires_grad_(True)), backprops the logit margin, and scores |grad| × |activation| — attribution patching over SAE features, grounded in Marks et al. (Sparse Feature Circuits) and Agrawal et al. (input vs. output features, which predicts precisely that correlational selection finds the wrong class).

200 of 32,768 features — 0.6% — carry about half the layer's knockout ceiling.

Ablation methodology review, and the repo reorganisation

v2 ablation runs across layers 0, 10, 11, 12, 13. A literature review checks the intervention against the field's known confounds and flags one real risk: replace mode discards the SAE reconstruction error ε, which Marks et al. keep as an explicit node. The test settles it — a pass-through baseline (SAE inserted, nothing zeroed) gives exactly 0.0000, and error-preserving delta mode gives 0.208 against replace mode's 0.213. Reconstruction error was not inflating the result, so replace stays.

Then an 18-commit reorganisation: core/ hooks/ training/ data/ feature_analysis/ ablation/, numbered pipeline/00–04, unnumbered tools/, Gen 0–2 code moved to archive/ intact. Any doc written before this cites dead paths — including README_SAE_EXPERIMENT.md and the LaTeX progress report.

The random controls were uniform, not matched

Configs asked for controls matched on matched_metric: "correct_mean" — a key that exists nowhere in the v2 stats files, which carry only causal_score, activation_mean, gradient_mean. _extract_metric_value returned None, fell through its whole chain, and _sample_matched_random_features silently took its uniform branch. Corroboration: 0 of 45 stored control features sat in the causal top-500, where matching predicts nearly all of them.

Every published z compared top-causal features against near-dead ones. Matched controls now perturb the residual stream more than the binding set and still produce a tenth of the effect, so the finding survives easily (Wilcoxon p = 2.2e-35), but every z in every pre-August document is inflated and must be restated. Margin drops and positive fractions are unaffected. The fix is matched_metric: "activation_mean" plus random_control.strict_matching: true, which raises instead of falling back.

Redundancy falsified; distribution found instead

A 47-condition matrix — joint, nested, leave-one-out, non-nested triples, budget-matched, downstream-knockout combinations, gates — all n=256 on the same question set, with matched controls throughout. The redundancy hypothesis had to be restated before it could be tested: nothing "relearns" at inference, so the only mechanism available is downstream layers re-reading the image. That is directly testable.

Test: ablate at layer 14, then knock out Image→Question at every layer downstream so re-reading is impossible. Additive prediction 0.5964; actual 0.5989. The ablation bites 0.2% harder with all re-reading blocked. Three further lines agree — the redundancy index sits inside the single-layer range (joint 72.0%, but layer 12 alone recovers 78.2%), the span's super-additivity is fully accounted for by the flow's own (ρ_A/ρ_K = 0.975), and leave-one-out points the wrong way for three of five layers.

Caveats that must survive into the report

  • Every pre-August z is inflated. Uniform controls, not matched. Margin drops and positive fractions are unaffected; restate the z-scores or cite the Wilcoxon values.
  • Layer 0 is a dictionary failure, not a negative result. dead_feature_fraction = 0.742 against 0.06–0.4% everywhere else. Its 4.4%-of-ceiling number says nothing about the model.
  • Layer 13 is a genuine puzzle. Knockout is inhibitory (−0.048) but ablating its causal features hurts (+0.122). The two interventions disagree in sign and "% of ceiling" is undefined. Worth a sentence, not a quiet omission.
  • Mean L0 is 1,100–1,600 across all six SAEs. Reconstruction is near-perfect (EV > 0.998) but the dictionaries are not very sparse — that limits claims about individual features being clean interpretable units.
  • Thin control arm in the multi-layer matrix. Only A0 and joint_L10-14 ran 15 control sets; the nested, non-nested and budget conditions used 1 and carry no z.
  • Saturation is unresolved, not absent. Spans of 1–5 layers are all in the linear regime; locating a knee needs wider spans.
  • The six-layer matched-control re-run was never done. Only layer 11 has a matched number; the per-layer table is still on the old uniform standard.
